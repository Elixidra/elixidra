About Mnara

A calm menu bar control center for your Mac.

Mnara keeps live system health close without feeling like a dashboard you have to manage. Open it when you need context, close it when you do not.

  • CPU, RAM, network, disk, battery, and temperature in one place
  • Designed for quick glances from the macOS menu bar
  • Local-first monitoring with a quiet, native feel

FAQ

Questions before you install?

Everything you need to know about buying, using, and updating Mnara.

Is Mnara a subscription?+

No. Mnara is sold as a simple one-time purchase for the current Mnara 1.x line.

How many Macs are included?+

A Mnara Lifetime License is intended for use on up to 3 personal Macs.

What macOS version is required?+

Mnara requires macOS 14 or newer and supports both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Does Mnara collect data?+

Mnara is designed as local-first monitoring. System health visibility stays focused on your Mac rather than a cloud dashboard.

Is there a refund policy?+

Yes. Mnara includes a 30-day money-back guarantee for personal purchases.

How do updates work?+

Mnara 1.x updates are included with the license. Update delivery remains handled by the existing Mnara release infrastructure.

Can I re-download Mnara later?+

Yes. Keep your purchase email and license details so you can download and activate Mnara again later.
