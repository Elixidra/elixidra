FAQ

A few useful answers.

Will Lask run on my Mac?

Lask requires macOS 12 or later and Apple Silicon. Intel Macs are not supported.

What does the licence include?

€29.99 once, for one person on up to 2 Macs, including every 1.x update.

Can I move my licence to another Mac?

Yes. Activating a third Mac is refused until one is deactivated. On a currently activated Mac, go to Settings → License → Deactivate.

Does Lask work offline?

Lask checks its licence about every 7 days when online. It keeps working for 21 days offline after the last successful check, then asks to check the licence again.

What is the radial menu shortcut?

The default on new installs is Control + Shift + Space (⌃⇧Space). Existing users keep their shortcut.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ask within 14 days of purchase by writing to lask@elixidra.com.

Does my content leave my Mac?

Clipboard content, screenshots and notes stay on your Mac. Release builds have no analytics or tracking. Network activity is limited to Lemon Squeezy licensing and Sparkle updates.
