Privacy Policy for LASK

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Overview

LASK is a local macOS productivity app. Your screenshots, notes, clipboard-related content, and editor work remain on your device unless you explicitly choose to send something outside the app.

What LASK Processes Locally

LASK is designed to work locally on your Mac. This includes:

  • Screenshots
  • Notes
  • Clipboard-related content
  • Editor work

Network Activity

LASK uses the network only for limited operational purposes:

  1. License activation, validation, and deactivation directly with Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com). The app sends the licence key and an instance name containing the Mac name, app version and a device identifier suffix on activation; validation and deactivation send the licence key and instance ID.
  2. App update checks and update downloads through Sparkle

Payments (Lemon Squeezy)

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy. LASK does not process card payments directly.

Sparkle Updates

LASK uses Sparkle to check for and download app updates. The appcast is hosted at https://github.com/Elixidra/lask-updates/releases/latest/download/appcast.xml; downloads use the URLs in that feed.

No Analytics or Tracking

LASK does not collect analytics, create usage identifiers or event logs, or use tracking or profiling. It keeps no debug logs of what you copy, capture or write.

Data Sharing

Data sharing is limited to service providers that are necessary to operate the app’s licensing and payment flow, and to deliver updates:

  • Lemon Squeezy (direct licence activation/validation/deactivation)
  • Lemon Squeezy (for checkout/payment handling)
  • Sparkle update infrastructure (for update checks/downloads)

Data Retention

Licensing and transaction-related records may be retained by relevant service providers and the app operator as reasonably needed for license management, support, accounting, legal compliance, and fraud prevention. Retention periods can vary based on operational and legal requirements.

Your Choices

You can choose whether to:

  • Activate a license
  • Deactivate a device license
  • Install available updates
  • Contact support with information you decide to share

If you do not want network activity related to licensing or updates, you can avoid activating or updating, but some app functions tied to licensing may be unavailable.

Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used for licensing and support operations. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be fully secure.
